# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Inferred from the public report and inquiry link: visitors evaluating DECAY HUNTERS trading strategies and their reported performance. The exact visitor segment has not been specified.

## Product Purpose

Let visitors inspect active strategies, their scheduled weekdays, capital, lot-scaled results, and performance across selected dates, then contact the team.

## Positioning

The public tracker brings weekly strategy schedules, per-strategy results, and combined performance history into one date-filterable report. No exclusive or comparative performance claim has been established.

## Operating Context

Visitors review the EOD report, filter the report by date and weekday, choose a strategy or all matching strategies, and inspect daily and monthly summaries. Lot multipliers scale the displayed capital and P&L in the report.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Active strategies carry capital, a weekday schedule, and a selectable lot multiplier.
- Report entries are grouped into daily and monthly P&L, history, and performance statistics.
- Displayed currency is Indian rupees.
- The page loads tracker records from the public API; preserve its loading, error, and empty states.
- Do not invent performance values, client outcomes, testimonials, or audit claims.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the DECAY HUNTERS name and existing logo. No specific color or typeface has been established as a binding brand requirement.

## Evidence on Hand

The public tracker API provides strategy settings and daily entries. The current record count changes over time and must not be hard-coded as a claim. No testimonials or independently verified performance claims are established.

## Product Principles

- Keep every performance figure grounded in tracker data.
- Make the date, weekday, and strategy scope of a figure clear.
- Let visitors compare strategies without losing their way in the report.
- Keep filters and key data usable by keyboard and on narrow screens.
